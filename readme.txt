Monitor and control a Gree Versati air-to-water heat pump directly over your local network. The app discovers or manually pairs the unit, reads heating and hot water telemetry, and exposes practical controls such as mode, target temperatures, Rapid hot water, Silence, W-depend, and Disinfect.

The integration works locally over UDP and does not require a cloud account. Use Homey Flow cards to react to mode, temperature, defrosting, Rapid, W-depend, and Disinfect changes, or to adjust supported heat pump settings from automations.
